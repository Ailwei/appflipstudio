CampusAmigo Account Deletion

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

You can request deletion of your CampusAmigo account and associated personal data at any time.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your CampusAmigo account, send an email to campusamigo.app@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Subject: CampusAmigo Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your CampusAmigo account in the message.

2. What Will Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and the personal information associated with it, including your account details and data stored in the app.

3. Processing Time

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days. We may retain limited information when required by law or when necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or enforce our agreements.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, contact us at campusamigo.app@gmail.com.
