CampusAmigo Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

CampusAmigo (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and authentication details provided through Google Sign-In or similar login methods.
  • Academic and personal information you provide in the app, including class schedules, assignments, revision tasks, exams, notifications, and preferences.
  • Device information such as device type, operating system, app version, and technical diagnostics needed to maintain and improve app performance.
  • Usage data, including interactions with features, timestamps, and app activity necessary for product improvement and support.
  • Notification-related data such as reminder preferences and push notification settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide login, account access, and personalized app functionality.
  • Manage reminders, class schedules, assignments, exams, and revision planning.
  • Improve app stability, performance, security, and user experience.
  • Send important service updates, account-related alerts, and notification reminders.
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Data Storage and Security

We store data using secure cloud services and industry-standard protection measures. Your information may be stored in Firebase or related backend infrastructure for authentication, data persistence, and notifications. While we take reasonable precautions, no system is completely immune to risk, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to support authentication, storage, analytics, and Push Notifications. These services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Examples include Google Sign-In and Firebase services.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the app securely and effectively.
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • To protect the safety, rights, or property of CampusAmigo, its users, or others.

6. Your Choices and Rights

You may manage certain preferences within the app, including notification settings and account access. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. If you want to exercise those rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children’s Privacy

CampusAmigo is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or legal basis. If you believe we may have collected information from a child, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be posted in the app or on the relevant website, and the “Effective date” above will be updated accordingly. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
