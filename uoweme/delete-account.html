U Owe Me Account Deletion

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

You can request deletion of your U Owe Me account and associated personal data at any time.

1. How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your U Owe Me account, send an email to appflipstudio@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Subject: U Owe Me Account Deletion Request

Email: appflipstudio@gmail.com

Please include your U Owe Me account email address in your request so that we can identify and verify the account.

For security purposes, we may need to verify that the request was made by the account owner before processing the deletion.

2. What Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete personal data associated with your U Owe Me account, subject to any information that we are legally required to retain.

This may include:

  • Your account and authentication information.
  • Your email address and profile information.
  • Debt and IOU records associated with your account.
  • Repayment and payment history stored in your account.
  • Other personal information associated with your U Owe Me account.

3. Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or comply with legal obligations.

Any information retained for these purposes will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose.

4. Deletion Processing

Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed after verification of the request. Once deletion has been completed, your account will no longer be available for normal use.

5. Important Information

Deleting your account may permanently remove your debt records, repayment history and other information stored in U Owe Me. Make sure you have exported or saved any information you may need before requesting account deletion.

6. Contact

For questions about account deletion or your personal data, contact AppFlipStudio at appflipstudio@gmail.com.
